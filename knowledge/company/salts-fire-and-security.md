## The business
- **Name:** Salts Fire and Security
- **Base:** Baildon, Shipley, West Yorkshire (serving Bradford, Leeds, Calderdale, Kirklees, Wakefield, North
  Yorkshire and beyond)
- **Director:** Alex Clancy
- **Website:** www.saltsfireandsecurity.co.uk (socials: LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok)
- **What we do:** design, installation, commissioning and maintenance of
  - fire detection and alarm systems (BS 5839-1 / BS 5839-6) - incl. Gent Vigilon, Kentec, Apollo-based systems
  - emergency lighting (BS 5266-1)
  - intruder and hold-up alarms (BS EN 50131 / PD 6662)
  - CCTV (BS EN 62676) and access control
  - fire extinguishers and other fire safety equipment
  - remedial works found on service visits, reactive call-outs and out-of-hours cover
- **Customers:** schools and academy trusts, councils, care homes, housing providers, commercial offices, retail,
  hospitality, logistics and industrial sites, and main contractors / electrical contractors needing a specialist.

## How the business runs
- **Recurring revenue:** maintenance contracts. A contract is renewed as a whole, so one contract only holds
  systems that share a renewal date; a site whose systems renew on different dates has one contract per date.
- **Work types in Salts FSM:** planned service visits (PPM), call-outs, remedials, installations, commissioning,
  surveys and quotes.
- **Service frequencies (typical):** fire alarm 6-monthly; emergency lighting monthly flick test by the client +
  annual full-duration test; monitored intruder alarms twice yearly; CCTV and access control annually;
  extinguishers annually.
- **Systems we use:** Salts FSM (our own field service management app on Azure), Microsoft 365 (Outlook,
  Teams, SharePoint), Sage for accounts.

## Signalling / switch-off
- All PSTN (analogue line) signalling units on maintained systems have already been upgraded, so the BT
  analogue switch-off is not an open risk or sales campaign for Salts.
